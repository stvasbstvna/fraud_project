# 2026 Cybersecurity Threat Context

Field Effect's 2026 outlook is included as current industry context, especially for discussion of AI-enhanced social engineering, exploitation of trust, identity compromise, and the continued importance of human-facing attack paths. It is a forecast and practitioner report, so claims drawn from it will be labeled as industry observations rather than experimental findings.

Its relevance to this project is the defensive implication: if generative systems make language cleaner and personalization easier, detectors should rely less on spelling errors and isolated urgency words. The planned mitigation tests therefore include intent/action signals, rewrite-aware augmentation, calibration, and optional consistency features.

Source: [2026 Cyber Threat Outlook](https://get.fieldeffect.com/hubfs/Resources/eBooks/2026%20Cyber%20Threat%20Outlook%20Report%20-%20Field%20Effect.pdf). A copy was present in the uploaded archive but is not committed here.
