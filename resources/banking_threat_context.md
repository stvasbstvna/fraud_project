# Banking Phishing Context

Banking scams are a useful narrow domain because the messages repeatedly exploit trust in account security, fraud alerts, identity verification, card use, and transfers. The Register.bank overview provides practitioner context on impersonation and authenticated bank identity. It is useful for explaining why claimed identity and domain consistency matter, but it is a stakeholder source and should not substitute for peer-reviewed evidence.

For this study, “banking-related” is an annotation decision about the pretext and target, not a keyword count. A legitimate transaction alert and a scam alert may share the same vocabulary. The separating evidence may be the requested action, destination, claimed sender, or inconsistency between identity and contact method.

Source: [Phishing: A Cybersecurity Threat Overview for Banks](https://register.bank/insights/phishing-overview-banking/).
