# Benchmark v2.1 dose-response power sensitivity

This prospective simulation uses seven repeated conditions and two correlated detector outcomes per frame, then applies Holm jointly to the seven new dose-response confirmatory hypotheses. Assumptions are not derived from v2.1 detector results.
